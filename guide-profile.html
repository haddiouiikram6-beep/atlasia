About Fatima

Hello! I am Fatima El Amrani, a proud native of Azilal in the heart of Morocco's High Atlas. For over 5 years, I have been guiding international travelers and outdoor enthusiasts through our hidden river trails, dramatic canyons, and ancient Berber villages.

My mission is to share the authentic spirit of the Béni Mellal-Khénifra region beyond the typical tourist paths. Whether we are trekking beneath the roaring cascades of Ouzoud, kayaking the turquoise waters of Bin El Ouidane, or sharing homemade bread with Berber families, your comfort, safety, and deep cultural connection come first.

🥾 High Atlas Mountain Trekking 🌊 Cascades d'Ouzoud Specialist 🏺 Amazigh Culture & History 🍲 Traditional Berber Gastronomy 🚑 Wilderness First Aid Certified
